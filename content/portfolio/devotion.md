She asked me to break my arm,  
I tore my shoulder from its socket.  
She asked me to throw myself from a bridge,  
I performed a graceful dive.

She asked me to feel her pain,  
I jammed a nail into my brain.  
She asked me to make her my only one,  
I dug their graves and walked away.

She asked me to give her my heart,  
I ripped it beating from my chest.  
She asked me to give her my soul,  
I sliced my neck and promised in death.
