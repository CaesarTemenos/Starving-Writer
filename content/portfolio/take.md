A forest of trees, glistened in white as shadows dance between. A laugh, a face, a brief moment of familiarity.

A hand outstretched which quickly fades, and turns to dust which scattered.

The moon's piercing pupil judges me with an unforgiving gaze. It gives, it takes, and delights in confusion.

A trail of blood lingers behind as every branch extended to pierce me. Scars like open wounds in the shape of an orchid.

I fell to my knees and looked to the skies for mercy. The crows laughed at my plight.

Give. Take.
