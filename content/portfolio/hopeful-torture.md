A tormenting spell. A disturbing agony cast upon the depths of my soul; my heart has usurped my will.

Forever allured to your mystifying eyes, a flower amidst thorns - a smile that dispersed the looming shadows.

For insanity has grasped my being and wrapped it in a ribbon made of your eluding love.

Your embrace has been etched into the very surface of my skin. Your laughter fills my lungs.

My touch aches to remember the outline of your hands. My lips cherish the memory of your kiss.

Forever bound by the chains of your beauty, treading on the shattered glass of my desire. A hopeful torture.
