The invisible laughing tormentors circle around my memories like vampires of feeling, in through the mind and out through the heart.

The ghosts of recent and of past serve as jailors to my own love and conviction. Down the spiral stairs into an abyss of my own design.

So I weigh these memories as fond or haunting, and the scales swing between, neither former nor latter; a testament to my own ruin.

And when, then, shall that release arrive, when those tormentors have consumed my soul and conscience?

And when, then, shall I be free of the light in her eyes, for which my heart so solemnly remembers?
