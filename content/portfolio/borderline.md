A fractured heart, a splintered mind. A soul that seeks to escape the confines of my flesh and chemicals. Forever misguided by vague signals it can never truly experience.

And there alone lies the concept of you; fleeting between heart and mind yet nestled in the soul. The mind wishes to comprehend you; to know you. The heart seeks your comfort in all the days it pulses.

But my soul claws from out of my chest to be nearer to yours. My soul feels your presence a hundred miles away, your fire in the arctic colds, your touch when you are not present.

Yet this trinity of experience and being - heart, mind, soul - all crave you nonetheless, and neither truly cancels out the other. A symphony of feeling and thought. An inexhaustible hunger.

A you-driven insanity.  
A borderline existence.
