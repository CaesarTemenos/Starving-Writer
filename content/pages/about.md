Seamus Caesar is a pen name. One I intend to use for my media presence (if there ever is one outside of this website) and published writing, digital or physical.

You'll understand if I find the notion of having my full real name readily available and tied to any of my potential work a little uneasy, especially with how small the internet has become. I'm not an entirely social person by nature, so it feels a little more secure this way.

Regardless, I should give you a little bit about me. No identifiable personal details, of course.

I'm from Northern Ireland, North of Ireland, whichever suits you. I'm in my early 20s, and from the time I was a first year at my secondary school, I found a comfort in writing. Fiction, non-fiction, whatever. It was the one thing I felt accomplished in, and actually happened to excel in. I find that distinction doesn't really correlate with my more recent, often mediocre work - but it was a start and served to enable my passion.

I particularly resonate with poetry which will likely serve as the majority of my writing. A comforting outlet for various unprocessed emotions. I'll get the hang of fiction one day, surely.

Either way, you can come back here to either ridicule or curiously follow along with my progress in pursuing my dreams. I hope you find something to resonate with here.

All the best,\
"Seamus"
